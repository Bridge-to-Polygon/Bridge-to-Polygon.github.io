How the estimate works

The fee is 0.6% of the amount plus 0.50 units, rounded to two decimals. The received amount is the input minus this fee, with a 1:1 unit conversion. Timing is 2–6 minutes plus one minute for each 1,000 units, rounded up and capped at 10 extra minutes.

How to bridge to Polygon

Connect a wallet, choose the asset and amount, and confirm. To bridge to Polygon you select the token on Ethereum, review the route, the fee and the amount that will arrive, then approve and sign. Assets appear on Polygon once the source transaction confirms, and nothing moves until you sign.

Fees and time to bridge to Polygon

The cost to bridge to Polygon is the Ethereum gas to send plus any protocol fee; transacting on Polygon afterwards is cheap because POL gas is low. Deposits settle after Ethereum confirmations, usually minutes. The quote shows the amount received before you commit.

What you can bridge to Polygon

You can bridge ETH, USDC, USDT, WBTC and other liquid assets to Polygon, where they work across its large DeFi ecosystem — DEXs, lending, gaming and liquidity. Availability depends on supported routes; the interface lists reachable assets and the expected amount for each.

Is it safe to bridge to Polygon?

A self-custody bridge keeps your keys in your wallet. Confirm the destination token's contract address, the amount received and the approval you grant, prefer a limited approval, and keep a little POL for gas. Treat any seed-phrase request as fraud.

Native Polygon bridge vs third-party routes

The native route bridges major assets directly between Ethereum and Polygon; third-party bridges can be faster or reach more chains, at the cost of another contract and fee. Judge any route by the net amount received and prefer audited options with deep liquidity.
Do I need POL to use assets on Polygon?
Yes. You pay Ethereum gas to bridge, and you need a little POL on Polygon to transact once your assets arrive.
How long does it take to bridge to Polygon?
Usually minutes, after your Ethereum transaction gets enough confirmations; busy periods take longer.
Which assets can I bridge to Polygon?
ETH, USDC, USDT, WBTC and other liquid tokens, depending on supported routes. The interface shows what is reachable and the amount received.
Do I need an account to bridge to Polygon?
No. You connect a self-custody wallet and bridge on-chain, with no sign-up and no custody.
What happens if my transfer fails?
A failed Ethereum transaction reverts and you keep your assets, though the gas for the attempt is spent.
